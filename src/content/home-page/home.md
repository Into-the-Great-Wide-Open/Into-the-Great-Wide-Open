We often live the majority of our lives within the well-defined boundaries of routines that offer familiarity and comfort. Travel is extraordinary in that it offers us an opportunity to escape those bounds and broaden our horizons. By exposing us to vibrant cultures, breathtaking scenery, and exotic cuisines, traveling can challenge not only our conceptions of the world around us but also our understanding of ourselves.

My travels have taken me to amazing places all around the world. They have allowed me to meet interesting people, savor new experiences, and test my own limits. In short, travel has changed my life in countless ways. I hope that the images and words detailing my own experiences will generate a similar curiosity and interest for others, leading them on their own journeys into the great wide open.
